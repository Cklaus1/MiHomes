# New Hire Onboarding Playbook (First 2 Weeks)

**Type**: onboarding  
**Owner**: Property Manager / EA  
**Applies to**: All new household staff  
**Duration**: 14 days structured onboarding

The goal of onboarding is not just information transfer — it's building the habits and judgment they'll need to operate independently. Follow this plan. Don't compress it.

---

## Before Day 1 (Manager checklist)

- [ ] Confirm start date, hours, and reporting location
- [ ] Prepare staff file: ID, tax forms, emergency contact, bank info
- [ ] Set up access: entry codes, alarm code, staff WhatsApp group
- [ ] Assign locker / storage area for personal items
- [ ] Print or share: this playbook, housekeeper playbook, communication playbook, employee manual
- [ ] Schedule Day 1 tour (1 hour blocked)
- [ ] Notify existing staff of new hire name and start date

---

## Day 1 — Orientation (Full Day with Manager)

### Morning: Welcome & Foundations (2 hours)
- [ ] Welcome conversation — learn their background, ask about prior experience
- [ ] Review role, hours, pay schedule, and reporting structure
- [ ] Cover non-negotiables: privacy, professionalism, communication
- [ ] Walk through employee manual — highlight key policies
- [ ] Sign required paperwork
- [ ] Introduce to all current staff

### Late Morning: Property Tour (1.5 hours)
- [ ] Walk every room — name and purpose of each space
- [ ] Show all key systems: HVAC panel, breaker, water shutoff, security
- [ ] Show supply storage locations (cleaning supplies, linens, paper goods)
- [ ] Show staff areas (break room, storage, personal lockers)
- [ ] Walk through any restricted areas and explain rules

### Afternoon: Systems Overview (2 hours)
- [ ] Review the emergency playbook — walk through each scenario, show key locations
- [ ] Show MiHomes task system: how tasks are assigned and marked done
- [ ] Show daily report format (communication playbook)
- [ ] Walk through the daily operations playbook — read it together
- [ ] Explain the weekly deep clean cycle

### End of Day 1
- [ ] Ask: "What questions do you have? What was unclear?"
- [ ] Set expectation: "Tomorrow you'll shadow [staff member] for the full morning routine"
- [ ] Confirm arrival time for Day 2

---

## Days 2–3 — Shadowing

**Goal**: Watch and absorb — not perform yet.

- [ ] Shadow senior staff through full morning opening checklist
- [ ] Shadow daily cleaning round in every room
- [ ] Watch kitchen and bathroom reset
- [ ] Watch laundry workflow
- [ ] Observe how issues are reported
- [ ] Observe end-of-day close routine

**Manager check-in (end of Day 3):**
- What felt clear?
- What felt uncertain?
- Any concerns or questions?

---

## Days 4–5 — Guided Practice

**Goal**: Do the work, with someone watching.

- [ ] New hire completes morning opening — senior staff observes silently
- [ ] New hire runs cleaning checklist — senior staff follows to verify each room
- [ ] New hire does laundry workflow independently
- [ ] New hire submits end-of-day report (first time — manager reviews before sending)

**Feedback at end of Day 5:**
- Walk through anything missed during the week
- Review checklist completion: what was done well, what needs work
- Set clear expectations for Week 2

---

## Week 2 — Independent Work with Oversight

**Goal**: Full independent operation, with spot checks.

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Day 6–7 | Full daily checklist independently |
| Day 8 | First weekly deep clean — one room type at a time |
| Day 9 | Full weekly deep clean independently |
| Day 10 | End-of-week review + 30-day expectations set |

### Daily during Week 2:
- [ ] Manager reviews daily report each evening
- [ ] Spot check one room per day (unannounced)
- [ ] Quick 5-min check-in at EOD — "any questions from today?"

### End of Week 2 review (30 min):
- [ ] Review performance against standards
- [ ] What's going well
- [ ] What still needs work — specific, actionable
- [ ] Set 30-day goal (one improvement focus)
- [ ] Confirm they feel confident and supported

---

## 30 / 60 / 90 Day Milestones

### 30 Days — Competency
- Runs full daily checklist without reminders
- Understands all cleaning standards
- Knows how to log issues in MiHomes
- Submits daily report correctly

### 60 Days — Consistency
- Quality is consistent across all rooms and all days
- No missed supply restock issues
- Proactively flags problems before they escalate
- Requires minimal oversight

### 90 Days — Ownership
- Treats the house as their own responsibility
- Suggests improvements to the playbook
- Can onboard the next new hire
- Is trusted to operate without daily check-ins

---

## Culture & Expectations (review on Day 1)

**Privacy is non-negotiable.**
You work in someone's home. What you see, hear, and know stays here. Do not discuss the property, the family, or anything you observe with anyone outside of work — including friends and family.

**Professionalism in a home setting.**
You are not a guest. Maintain a professional demeanor at all times. Don't use household amenities (TV, food, seating areas) unless explicitly offered.

**Communication.**
When in doubt, ask. A question is never a problem. Silence when something's unclear always is.

**Boundaries.**
- Do not give out the property address, entry codes, or any access information to anyone
- Do not bring guests to the property — ever
- Do not photograph the interior of the property

**The standard is the standard.**
"Good enough" is not the standard. The checklist defines the standard. If you can't meet it today (sick, short on time, supplies low), communicate it — don't silently deliver less.
