# Separation & Offboarding Playbook

**Type**: hr  
**Owner**: EA (Millena)  
**Properties**: all  
**Triggers**: resignation, performance termination, conduct violation, immediate termination

To ensure all employee separations are handled professionally, consistently, and securely — protecting the household, maintaining dignity for the employee, and minimizing operational disruption.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Respectful and discreet at all times
- Clear and documented decisions
- Consistent process across all staff
- Protect privacy and security of the household
- Act promptly when necessary

---

## Types of Separation

| Type | Phase 1 Required? | Notice Period |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Voluntary (resignation) | No | Per offer letter |
| Involuntary — Performance | Yes | Per documentation |
| Involuntary — Conduct / Policy violation | Situational | Immediate or notice |
| Immediate Termination (gross misconduct) | No | None |

---

## Phase 1: Performance Management (skip for immediate terminations)

### Step 1: Issue Identification
- [ ] Document what happened, when, and the impact on household operations
- [ ] Confirm this rises to a level requiring formal action (not just coaching)
- [ ] Notify Chris before proceeding

### Step 2: Verbal Warning
- [ ] Private conversation — never in front of other staff
- [ ] Clearly state: the issue, the expected standard, the timeline for improvement
- [ ] Document the conversation (date, what was said, employee response)

### Step 3: Written Warning
- [ ] Prepare written document including:
  - Specific concerns with dates and examples
  - Required changes and measurable expectations
  - Improvement deadline
  - Consequences if not met
- [ ] Review with Chris before issuing
- [ ] Deliver in person, provide a copy, retain signed acknowledgment

### Step 4: Final Review
- [ ] Evaluate improvement against the written warning benchmarks
- [ ] Decision: return to good standing OR proceed to termination
- [ ] Document decision and reasoning regardless of outcome

---

## Phase 2: Termination Decision

### When Termination Is Triggered
- Repeated performance issues after documented warnings
- Failure to meet expectations within improvement timeline
- Policy violations (especially privacy or confidentiality)
- Breach of trust
- Safety or security concern
- Immediate: theft, serious misconduct, privacy breach

### Pre-Termination Checklist
- [ ] Documentation reviewed and complete
- [ ] Decision aligned with Chris
- [ ] Final pay amount calculated (confirm state law requirements)
- [ ] Access removal plan prepared (see Phase 4)
- [ ] Termination meeting scheduled — private, in-person preferred
- [ ] Replacement / coverage plan in place before meeting

---

## Phase 3: Termination Meeting

**Keep it brief, clear, and respectful. This is not a negotiation.**

### Before
- [ ] Choose a private setting — never in a common area
- [ ] Have a witness present (EA or manager) when possible
- [ ] Have any paperwork (final pay, return of property receipt) ready

### Structure
1. **Direct opening statement**: "We've made the decision to end your employment, effective [date]."
2. **Brief reason** (if appropriate): keep it high-level and factual — no elaboration
3. **Next steps**: final pay, return of property, access removal, personal belongings
4. **Close**: thank them for their time, wish them well

### Rules
- Do not argue
- Do not negotiate in the moment
- Do not discuss other staff members
- Do not over-explain or justify repeatedly
- If they become upset: stay calm, stay quiet, let them process

---

## Phase 4: Immediate Security Actions (CRITICAL)

**In a private residence, this is non-negotiable. Do this same day — ideally before or during the meeting.**

### Collect
- [ ] All keys (house, mailbox, storage)
- [ ] Keycards or fobs
- [ ] Garage remotes
- [ ] Any household property (phone, equipment, uniform items if applicable)

### Disable
- [ ] Alarm codes (change or remove their code)
- [ ] Smart home / app access
- [ ] WiFi password (change if shared)
- [ ] MiHomes or any internal system access
- [ ] Any cameras or monitoring they had access to

### If Remote Termination
- [ ] Disable all digital access before or during the call
- [ ] Arrange return of physical items within 24–48 hours (with confirmation)
- [ ] Send written confirmation of termination and return instructions

> **Note**: For immediate terminations involving misconduct or trust violations — disable access first, then conduct the meeting.

---

## Phase 5: Offboarding Process

### Employee Responsibilities
- [ ] Return all household property (documented on receipt)
- [ ] Remove personal belongings — coordinate a specific time (with staff present)

### Employer Responsibilities
- [ ] Final paycheck issued per state law timeline
- [ ] PTO payout (if applicable per offer letter)
- [ ] Any required documentation (separation letter if requested)
- [ ] Confirm mailing address for any final correspondence

### Optional
- [ ] Brief exit conversation — only if separation is voluntary and relationship is good
- [ ] Collect feedback on household operations (voluntary, no pressure)

---

## Phase 6: Household Reset

- [ ] Reassign responsibilities immediately — do not leave gaps
- [ ] Notify remaining staff (minimal, need-to-know only): *"[Name] is no longer with the household. Here's how we're handling coverage..."*
- [ ] Adjust schedules and coverage plan
- [ ] Review: does the role need to be backfilled, restructured, or absorbed?
- [ ] If backfilling: trigger the Hiring Playbook

---

## Phase 7: Documentation (retain indefinitely)

Maintain an internal record of:
- [ ] Type of separation and effective date
- [ ] Dates of any warnings issued (if applicable)
- [ ] Notes from termination meeting (brief factual summary)
- [ ] Signed return of property receipt
- [ ] Access removal checklist — confirmed complete
- [ ] Final pay confirmation

Store in: `knowledge/staff/offboarding/<name>-<date>.md`

## Special Situations

### Immediate Termination (No Warning)

**Used for**: theft, breach of privacy/confidentiality, safety risk, serious misconduct

**Process:**
1. Disable access immediately (before or during meeting)
2. Terminate in person with a witness if at all possible
3. Escort off property if needed — do not leave them unattended
4. Document thoroughly — same day
5. Notify Chris immediately

### Voluntary Resignation

1. Confirm resignation in writing (email or text is fine)
2. Determine final date — honor notice period at your discretion
3. Run standard offboarding (Phases 4–7)
4. Decide whether to have them work the notice period or pay in lieu
5. If trust is intact: standard transition. If trust is questionable: end sooner.

## At-a-Glance Checklist

**Before the meeting:**
- [ ] Documentation complete
- [ ] Final pay prepared
- [ ] Access removal plan ready
- [ ] Coverage plan in place

**During the meeting:**
- [ ] Clear, respectful communication
- [ ] Keep it brief
- [ ] Witness present if possible

**After the meeting:**
- [ ] Keys and physical items collected
- [ ] All digital access disabled
- [ ] Staff notified (minimal)
- [ ] Duties reassigned
- [ ] Documentation finalized and filed

## Household-Specific Notes

In a private residence, speed and discretion matter more than corporate formality.

**If trust is broken:**
- Move quickly — do not delay out of discomfort
- Lock down access immediately — this protects the family
- Keep communication tight — staff don't need details, just direction

**Privacy**: zero tolerance for breaches, ever  
**Access control**: treat like a security event, not just HR  
**Discretion**: the family's privacy is protected at all times, even in how the termination is communicated
