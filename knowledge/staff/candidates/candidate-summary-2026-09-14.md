## The One Concern — Schedule

Her other job runs Mon–Wed, 12–7. Her Belle window is mornings only, hard stop at noon —
roughly **17.5 hrs/week**.

- **Daily cleaning fits** (2–3 hrs needed, she has 3.5).
- **The weekly deep clean does not** — that is 4–6 hours and cannot happen before a hard noon
  handoff to another employer.
- **No buffer**, and **October is exposed**: Marcia is away two weeks, and the noon ceiling caps
  exactly the surge coverage we would want.

**One question resolves it.** She called the other job "mostly Monday through Wednesday" but
limited herself to "before 12" all five days. If Thursday and Friday afternoons are open, the
deep clean has somewhere to live. If not, we scope her as daily-maintenance-only and deep cleans
stay with Marcia. Both workable — but decided before an offer, not after.

## What We Have Not Established

The screen was deliberately abbreviated — we had already spoken to her references — so we have
**no evidence on her cleaning methodology**. Two related caveats: her current household employs a
separate housekeeper for the full-house clean (she volunteered this rather than overselling), and

**That is what the trial day is for** — a real assessment, not a formality: standard cleaning
round, minimal guidance, and the two questions we never asked (how she knows a room is clean,
what she does when she finds something broken).
