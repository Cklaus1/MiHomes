## Why She Is Worth a Trial Day

**1. References are already done, and they are good.**
Two employers, two years, both recommending her specifically for housekeeping. Every candidate
we advanced in May was ranked on interview impression alone — references came later, or not at
all. This is the first time we have had confirmation before the recommendation.

**2. She is inside our comp range.**
$25/hr. Brandi was $30–40 (above our ceiling), Sherri ~$30, Tinuola $30–35. Comp was the reason
two of our May candidates stalled. It is not an issue here.

**3. She has no crew and no company.**
This was the disqualifier that removed several candidates in the spring — people who wanted to
bring their own teams into the house. Jen works alone, brings no supplies, and uses whatever the
household provides. She raised the distinction herself, unprompted, having worked out that it
mattered to us.

**4. She already works inside a multi-staff household.**
Her current placement has a separate housekeeper, a tutor, and repair crews. She coordinates
around them without friction. Our concern with Zion in May was whether a business owner could
take direction from Marcia day-to-day — that risk does not apply here.

**5. She takes household-specific direction and retains it.**
The clearest example from the call: her employer taught her to skip fabric softener on technical
fabrics so the material is not degraded, and to wash delicates separately. She repeated the
instruction accurately and unprompted, and closed with *"I'll just cater to however the family
would want me to do so."* That is the correct posture for household staff — following the house's
standard rather than importing her own.

**6. Cleanest background of anyone we have screened.**
No stop-level concerns of any kind. For comparison: Brandi had two felonies requiring your
decision, Zion the direction-taking risk, Sherri and Tinuola were both over our ceiling.

**7. She lives down the street.**
Commute reliability is a non-issue, and she is a known quantity to a neighbouring household.
She responded to our disclosure of past staff theft without defensiveness and is comfortable
working under camera monitoring.

## The One Real Concern — Schedule

Her hours and ours do not overlap as cleanly as they first appear.

| | |
|---|---|
| Her other job | Mon–Wed (occasionally Thu), 12:00–7:00 → ~21 hrs |
| Her Belle window | Mon–Fri, hard stop before 12:00 → realistically 8:00–11:30 |
| Belle capacity | ~17.5 hrs/week |

- **Daily cleaning fits** — the playbook allows 2–3 hours, and she has 3.5.
- **The weekly deep clean does not.** That is a 4–6 hour job and it cannot happen in a morning
  block that ends at a hard noon handoff to another employer.
- **There is no buffer.** If anything at Belle runs long, she is late to the other family.
- **October is the exposure.** Marcia is away two weeks. The noon ceiling caps exactly the surge
  coverage we would want from a second housekeeper.

**One question resolves most of this.** She described the other job as "mostly Monday through
Wednesday," but limited herself to "before 12" on all five days. If Thursday and Friday
afternoons are genuinely open, the deep clean has somewhere to live and the role works as
scoped. If they are not, we scope her as daily-maintenance-only and the weekly deep clean stays
with Marcia.

Both outcomes are workable. It simply has to be decided before an offer, not after.

## What We Have Not Yet Established

The phone screen was deliberately abbreviated — we had already spoken to her references and did
not run the full question set. As a result we have **no evidence on her cleaning methodology**.
She confirmed she can handle marble, wood and other surfaces, but described no method.

Two related caveats:

- Her most recent work is closer to family assistance than deep cleaning. Her current household
  employs a separate housekeeper every other Monday for the full-house clean. She volunteered
  this herself rather than overselling her scope, which I read as a point in her favour — but it
  means the depth of her cleaning experience is unproven.
- She has not worked anywhere near Belle's scale and could not give square footage for any home
  she has worked in.

**This is what the trial day is for.** I would not treat it as a formality. I will run it as a
genuine assessment — standard cleaning round, minimal guidance, and the two questions we never
asked: how she knows a room is actually clean, and what she does when she finds something broken.
