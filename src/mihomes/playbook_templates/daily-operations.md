# Daily Operations

**Type**: recurring
**Owner**: [House manager]
**Estimated time**: 1–2 hours spread across the day

The daily rhythm that keeps [Property name] running smoothly. Open the house in the morning, keep things on track during the day, and close it securely at night.

---

## Morning Open
- [ ] Walk the property inside and out and note anything out of place
- [ ] Disarm the alarm and check the security system shows no overnight alerts
- [ ] Check heating, cooling, and hot water are working as expected
- [ ] Look for leaks, drips, or damp under sinks and around appliances
- [ ] Review today's calendar for deliveries, visitors, vendors, and household plans
- [ ] Confirm staff attendance and assign the day's priorities
- [ ] Check messages and voicemail received overnight and reply to anything urgent

## Throughout the Day
- [ ] Receive deliveries, check them against the order, and put items away
- [ ] Meet vendors on arrival and confirm the scope of work before they start
- [ ] Inspect completed vendor work before they leave and note any follow-ups
- [ ] Log new maintenance issues as soon as they are found
- [ ] Update open tasks with progress and blockers
- [ ] Restock household supplies that fall below the agreed level
- [ ] Collect the post and sort it for the household

## Outdoors
- [ ] Check gates, fences, and outdoor lighting for damage
- [ ] Clear leaves, debris, or rubbish from entrances and paths
- [ ] Check the pool, spa, or water features if the property has them
- [ ] Confirm the irrigation system ran and nothing is waterlogged or dry

## Evening Close
- [ ] Lock all external doors, windows, and garage doors
- [ ] Turn off unneeded lights, appliances, and outdoor equipment
- [ ] Set the thermostat to its overnight setting
- [ ] Arm the alarm and confirm it shows as set
- [ ] Take out rubbish and recycling on collection days

## End-of-Day Handover
- [ ] Write a short daily summary of what was done and what is outstanding
- [ ] Flag anything urgent for tomorrow to [Estate manager]
- [ ] Update the calendar with any new appointments made today

---

## Reference
- When the household is away, add a mid-day walkthrough and keep curtains and lights on a lived-in routine.
- Anything affecting safety or security is escalated immediately, not left for the daily summary.
