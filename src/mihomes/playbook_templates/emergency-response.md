# Emergency Response

**Type**: emergency
**Owner**: All staff
**Estimated time**: Immediate response; 1–3 hours to stabilise and report

What to do when something goes seriously wrong at [Property name]. People first, then the property, then the paperwork. Call for help before you try to fix anything.

---

## Immediate Response (Any Emergency)
- [ ] Make sure everyone is safe and move people away from the danger
- [ ] Call emergency services if anyone is hurt or at risk
- [ ] Notify [Emergency contact] with what happened and where
- [ ] Notify [House manager] or [Estate manager] as soon as people are safe
- [ ] Account for every person who was on the property

## Fire or Smoke
- [ ] Evacuate everyone to the agreed assembly point without stopping for belongings
- [ ] Call the fire service from a safe place outside the building
- [ ] Keep everyone out of the building until the fire service says it is safe
- [ ] Meet the fire service on arrival and tell them who may still be inside

## Water Leak or Flood
- [ ] Shut off the main water valve at [Water shut-off location]
- [ ] Turn off electricity to affected areas if it is safe to reach the panel
- [ ] Move valuables, documents, and electronics away from the water
- [ ] Call [Emergency plumber] to stop and repair the leak
- [ ] Photograph all water damage before anything is cleaned up

## Power Outage
- [ ] Check whether neighbours have power to tell a local fault from a wider outage
- [ ] Check the main electrical panel for tripped breakers
- [ ] Report the outage to the electricity provider
- [ ] Confirm the backup generator has started if the property has one
- [ ] Unplug sensitive electronics to protect them when power returns

## Gas Smell
- [ ] Evacuate everyone immediately without using switches, phones, or flames inside
- [ ] Call the gas emergency line from outside the property
- [ ] Shut off gas at the meter only if it is safe and you have been trained to

## Break-In or Security Threat
- [ ] Leave the property or move to a safe room if an intruder may still be inside
- [ ] Call the police and report the address and what you have seen
- [ ] Avoid touching anything in affected areas until the police have finished
- [ ] List missing or damaged items once the police allow access

## Medical Emergency
- [ ] Call emergency services and describe the person's condition clearly
- [ ] Give first aid only to the level you are trained for
- [ ] Send someone to open the gate and meet the ambulance

## After the Emergency
- [ ] Write an incident report covering what happened, when, and who responded
- [ ] Call the insurance claim line and record the claim number
- [ ] Book repairs and log them as work orders against the property
- [ ] Hold a short debrief with staff to agree what to improve next time
- [ ] Update this playbook with anything learned
