# Hiring

**Type**: one-off
**Owner**: [Estate manager]
**Estimated time**: 2–6 weeks

How to fill a household staff role, from defining the job to signing an offer. Run it once per open position.

---

## Define the Role
- [ ] Write down the duties, schedule, and properties the role will cover
- [ ] Agree the pay range, benefits, and any live-in or travel arrangements
- [ ] List the must-have skills, licences, and languages for the role
- [ ] Get approval from [Household principal] for the role and budget
- [ ] Write the job description from the agreed duties and requirements

## Source Candidates
- [ ] Decide which channels to use, such as agencies, referrals, or job boards
- [ ] Post the job description to each chosen channel
- [ ] Brief any staffing agency on the role, budget, and timeline
- [ ] Ask trusted staff and contacts for referrals

## Screen
- [ ] Review applications against the must-have requirements
- [ ] Hold a short phone screen with each shortlisted candidate
- [ ] Confirm availability, pay expectations, and right to work during the screen
- [ ] Select candidates to invite for a full interview

## Interview
- [ ] Prepare a consistent list of interview questions for the role
- [ ] Interview each candidate and record notes straight afterwards
- [ ] Ask about discretion and confidentiality in every interview
- [ ] Arrange a paid working trial for the strongest candidates
- [ ] Gather feedback on each trial from [House manager] and other staff

## Check and Decide
- [ ] Contact at least two references for the preferred candidate
- [ ] Run the background check the candidate has consented to
- [ ] Verify licences, certifications, or driving record if the role needs them
- [ ] Agree the final choice with [Household principal]

## Offer
- [ ] Send a written offer with pay, schedule, start date, and probation period
- [ ] Send the confidentiality agreement for signature with the offer
- [ ] Confirm the signed offer and agreement have been returned
- [ ] Let unsuccessful candidates know politely that the role is filled
- [ ] Start the onboarding playbook for the new hire

---

## Reference
- Keep candidate information private and delete it once it is no longer needed.
- Ask the same core questions of every candidate so they can be compared fairly.
- Follow local employment law on interview questions, background checks, and contracts.
