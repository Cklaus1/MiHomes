# Household Communication

**Type**: recurring
**Owner**: [House manager]
**Estimated time**: 30–60 minutes per week

How staff keep the household informed without overwhelming them. Clear updates, the right channel for each kind of message, and nothing important falling through the cracks.

---

## Set Up the Channels
- [ ] Agree with the household which channel to use for urgent, routine, and FYI messages
- [ ] Agree quiet hours when only urgent messages should be sent
- [ ] Create a staff group chat for day-to-day coordination
- [ ] Share the contact list so every staff member knows who to reach for what
- [ ] Confirm who has authority to approve spending and up to what amount

## Daily
- [ ] Send the household a short update only when something needs their attention
- [ ] Reply to household requests promptly and confirm when each one is done
- [ ] Log every household request as a task so it is tracked to completion
- [ ] Share the day's priorities with staff at the start of each shift

## Weekly
- [ ] Send the household a weekly summary of completed work, open issues, and upcoming plans
- [ ] List decisions you need from the household with a clear yes or no question for each
- [ ] Share the coming week's calendar of vendors, deliveries, and staff schedules
- [ ] Hold a short staff meeting to review last week and plan the next one
- [ ] Follow up on any request that has been open for more than a week

## Before Household Arrivals
- [ ] Confirm arrival time, number of guests, and any special requests
- [ ] Brief staff on arrival plans and who is responsible for what
- [ ] Send the household a short note confirming the house is ready

## When Something Goes Wrong
- [ ] Tell the household about the problem quickly, even before it is fully solved
- [ ] Explain what happened, what you are doing about it, and when it will be fixed
- [ ] Send a follow-up message once the problem is resolved

---

## Reference
- Lead with what the household needs to know or decide, then give the detail.
- One topic per message makes requests easier to answer and track.
- Never share household information, photos, or schedules outside the staff team.
- Urgent means safety, security, or serious damage. Everything else can wait for the summary.
