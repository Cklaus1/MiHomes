# Housekeeping Standards

**Type**: recurring
**Owner**: [Head housekeeper]
**Estimated time**: 4–6 hours per full house clean

The standard every room should meet after a full clean. Use it to train new housekeepers and to run a consistent weekly clean of [Property name].

---

## Before You Start
- [ ] Review the household calendar for guests, events, or rooms that must not be disturbed
- [ ] Check the notes log for special requests or issues reported since the last clean
- [ ] Stock the cleaning caddy with products, cloths, and gloves for each surface type
- [ ] Confirm which rooms are occupied and agree a cleaning order with [House manager]

## Bedrooms
- [ ] Strip and remake beds with fresh linen, hospital corners, and pillows plumped
- [ ] Dust all surfaces top to bottom, including headboards, lamps, and picture frames
- [ ] Wipe light switches, door handles, and remote controls with disinfectant
- [ ] Empty bins and replace liners in every bedroom
- [ ] Vacuum carpets and rugs, including under beds and behind doors
- [ ] Tidy wardrobes and drawers only where the household has asked for it

## Bathrooms
- [ ] Clean and disinfect toilets inside and out, including the base and hinges
- [ ] Descale and polish showers, taps, and glass screens until streak-free
- [ ] Wipe down vanities, mirrors, and cabinet fronts
- [ ] Replace towels, bath mats, and hand towels with fresh sets
- [ ] Restock toilet paper, soap, and toiletries to the agreed par levels
- [ ] Mop bathroom floors and dry them so no one slips

## Kitchen
- [ ] Wipe and sanitise countertops, splashbacks, and the sink
- [ ] Clean the outside of appliances and the hob or cooktop
- [ ] Check the fridge for expired food and wipe any spills
- [ ] Empty kitchen bins, rinse the bins, and replace liners
- [ ] Sweep and mop the kitchen floor, including under the edges of cabinets

## Living and Common Areas
- [ ] Dust shelves, ornaments, and electronics with the correct cloth for each surface
- [ ] Plump cushions, fold throws, and straighten furniture to its standard position
- [ ] Clean glass tables, mirrors, and interior glass doors
- [ ] Vacuum upholstery and rugs, and mop or polish hard floors
- [ ] Wipe skirting boards and window sills in one room per visit on rotation

## Finishing
- [ ] Walk every room once more to check against this standard
- [ ] Report any damage, breakages, or maintenance issues to [House manager]
- [ ] Note low stock of cleaning supplies or household consumables on the shopping list
- [ ] Wash, dry, and put away cleaning cloths and mop heads
- [ ] Log the clean as complete with any notes for the next visit

---

## Reference
- Always test a new product on a hidden area before using it on stone, wood, or fabric.
- Never mix bleach with other cleaning products.
- Handle valuables, artwork, and antiques only as instructed by [House manager].
- Keep private documents, devices, and personal items exactly where they were found.
