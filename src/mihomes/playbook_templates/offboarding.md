# Offboarding

**Type**: one-off
**Owner**: [Estate manager]
**Estimated time**: 1–3 days

How to close out a staff member's employment cleanly and securely, whether they resign or are let go. Security steps happen on the last day, not later.

---

## Plan the Departure
- [ ] Confirm the last working day in writing with the staff member
- [ ] Check the contract and local law for notice, final pay, and holiday owed
- [ ] Decide who will take over each of their duties
- [ ] Brief the household and other staff on the change and the handover plan

## Knowledge Handover
- [ ] Ask the departing staff member to list their recurring duties and routines
- [ ] Collect notes on household preferences, vendors, and open issues they manage
- [ ] Hold a handover session with whoever is taking over their duties
- [ ] Reassign their open tasks and work orders in the app

## Return of Property
- [ ] Collect all keys, access cards, and garage remotes using the issued-items record
- [ ] Collect uniforms, phones, laptops, and any other equipment
- [ ] Check that nothing belonging to the household leaves with them

## Revoke Access
- [ ] Change alarm codes and gate codes they knew
- [ ] Change door locks or re-key if any key cannot be accounted for
- [ ] Deactivate their app account and remove their property access
- [ ] Remove them from staff group chats and shared calendars
- [ ] Change shared Wi-Fi and account passwords they had access to
- [ ] Notify the security company and regular vendors that they have left

## Close Out
- [ ] Process final pay, including any holiday or notice owed
- [ ] Remind them in writing of their ongoing confidentiality obligations
- [ ] Hold an exit conversation and record any useful feedback
- [ ] Update the staff contact list and emergency contact sheet
- [ ] File their employment records securely
- [ ] Start the hiring playbook if the role needs to be refilled

---

## Reference
- If the departure is not amicable, revoke access before or at the start of the final conversation.
- Keep the conversation private, respectful, and factual.
