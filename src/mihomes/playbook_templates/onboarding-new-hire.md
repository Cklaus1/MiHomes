# Onboarding a New Hire

**Type**: one-off
**Owner**: [House manager]
**Estimated time**: 1–2 weeks

Everything a new staff member needs to be safe, productive, and trusted in their first two weeks. Start it as soon as the offer is signed.

---

## Before Day One
- [ ] Collect the signed contract, confidentiality agreement, and right-to-work documents
- [ ] Add the new hire to payroll with their bank and tax details
- [ ] Order uniforms or work clothing in the right sizes
- [ ] Prepare keys, access cards, or alarm codes the role needs
- [ ] Create their app account and give them access to the right properties
- [ ] Add them to the staff group chat and contact list
- [ ] Send a welcome message with start time, address, parking, and who to ask for

## Day One
- [ ] Welcome the new hire and introduce them to the household and other staff
- [ ] Give a full tour of the property, including areas that are off limits
- [ ] Show them every emergency exit, shut-off valve, and the assembly point
- [ ] Walk through the emergency response playbook together
- [ ] Hand over keys, codes, and equipment and record what was issued
- [ ] Explain house rules on privacy, phones, photos, and guests

## First Week
- [ ] Walk through the daily operations playbook for their role
- [ ] Train them on the housekeeping standards if cleaning is part of the role
- [ ] Show them how to log issues and update tasks in the app
- [ ] Pair them with an experienced staff member for each core duty
- [ ] Explain how household preferences are recorded and where to find them
- [ ] Check in at the end of each day for questions

## First Two Weeks
- [ ] Have them complete core duties on their own while you review the results
- [ ] Hold a two-week review covering what is going well and what needs work
- [ ] Agree written goals for the rest of the probation period
- [ ] Book the end-of-probation review in the calendar

---

## Reference
- Keep a record of every key, code, and device issued so offboarding is simple.
- Give alarm codes and safe access only to roles that genuinely need them.
