The homes are the easy part. What makes an estate hard to run is everything
attached to them — the contracts that renew, the vendor nobody can remember the
name of, the warranty that expires in eleven months.

If you add one contract with its renewal date, MiHomes will tell you about it
before it lapses. That is the whole trick, and it works for exactly as many
things as you put in.
